What We Run

Six ways to start building

From your first pitch to your first cheque — structured programs at every stage of the founder journey.

01

E-Summit

Our flagship two-day gathering of founders, investors, and operators — talks, panels, and a marketplace floor.

02

Startup Bootcamp

A six-week sprint from idea to MVP, with weekly mentor office hours and a demo day.

03

Hackathon

24 hours, real problems from partner companies, and a judging panel that hires.

04

Founder Talks

Monthly fireside chats with alumni founders — unfiltered stories about what actually happened.

05

Pitch Competition

Ten minutes, five judges, one term sheet on the line for the winning team.

06

Mentorship Program

Paired 1:1 mentorship with industry operators for one full semester.

About Us

Building the founders of tomorrow

The Entrepreneurship Cell of BITS Vizag exists to make starting something feel possible — and give students the room, mentors, and pressure-testing to actually do it.

Mission

To give every student at BITS Vizag a real, practical path from idea to execution — through mentorship, programs, and a community that takes building seriously.

Vision

To make BITS Vizag a campus known for producing founders, not just employees — where starting a company is a normal, supported choice.

Objectives

Run high-quality programs year-round, connect students to operators and investors, and lower the barrier to a first pitch, first prototype, and first customer.

Innovation Ecosystem

We partner with alumni founders, local incubators, and industry mentors to bring the outside startup world onto campus.

2019

Founded

E-Cell launches with a handful of students and one workshop series.

2021

First E-Summit

Our flagship event brings outside founders and investors to campus for the first time.

2023

Mentorship Program launched

1:1 pairing with industry operators becomes a full semester-long track.

2026

850+ members

E-Cell becomes one of the largest student communities on campus.
